# 🛡️ Privacy Policy

ScreenRest is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device.

## Information We Store
ScreenRest stores only the settings you choose inside the app, including:

- selected blocked apps  
- filter settings  
- schedules  
- permission states  

These settings remain entirely on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

## Information We Do Not Collect
ScreenRest does not collect:

- personal data  
- app usage statistics  
- analytics  
- identifiers  
- location data  
- network data  
- crash logs  
- advertising data  

We do not use external servers, cloud storage, or third‑party analytics.

## Permissions
ScreenRest uses certain Android permissions to provide its features:

- **Usage Access:** to detect when selected apps are opened  
- **Notification Access:** to observe background app launches  
- **Display Over Other Apps:** to show block screens and filters  
- **Precise Scheduling:** to activate blocks and filters at exact times  
- **Accessibility Service:** to apply filters on the lock screen and in restricted areas. ScreenRest uses the Android Accessibility Service API strictly on-device to draw eye-comfort screen filters over the lock screen and system windows. We do not use this service to log keystrokes, capture sensitive personal content, or bypass user privacy controls.

These permissions are used only for the features described and never for data collection.

## Children’s Privacy
ScreenRest does not collect any personal information and can be used by all ages.  
However, ScreenRest is not intended as parental‑control software.
